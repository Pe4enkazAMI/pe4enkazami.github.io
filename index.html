Ian Maksimov — Research in motion

Six independent i-SIR chains, labelled A through F, run in parallel. Each chain retains its own current point, draws seven independent proposals, normalizes its eight importance weights, and makes its own categorical selection. There is one highlighted state per chain. At selection, all six states switch directly to their chosen points and softly bubble in place, including any retained points. All six selected indices are displayed together. Only the detailed weights and categorical view rotates between chains. The target has two modes within a bounded plane. Use the motion button to pause the illustration.
